## Purpose

Backlog Refinement (formerly Grooming) is an ongoing process where the Product Owner and Development Team collaborate to ensure Product Backlog items are well-understood, properly sized, and ready for future sprints.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- Estimate story points for upcoming items
- Break down large items into smaller, actionable stories
- Ensure items meet the Definition of Ready
- Maintain a healthy "ready" backlog (2-3 sprints ahead)

## Definition of Ready Checklist

For each refined item, verify:
- [ ] User story follows proper format
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are clear and testable
- [ ] Story is estimated
- [ ] Dependencies are identified
- [ ] Design/mockups available (if needed)
- [ ] Technical approach is understood
- [ ] Story fits within single sprint
- [ ] No outstanding questions
