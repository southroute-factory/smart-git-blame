## Purpose

The Sprint Retrospective provides an opportunity for the Scrum Team to inspect itself and create a plan for improvements during the next Sprint. This is a safe space for honest reflection and continuous improvement.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Identify what went well to continue
- Identify what needs improvement
- Create actionable improvement items
- Foster team cohesion and psychological safety
