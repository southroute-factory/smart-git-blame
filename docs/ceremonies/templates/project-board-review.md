## Purpose

The Project Board Review is a weekly meeting to inspect the state of work in progress, ensure board hygiene, identify bottlenecks, and maintain visibility into sprint health. This is not a status meeting—it's a board health check.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Ensure all work is accurately reflected on the board
- Identify stale or blocked items
- Verify WIP limits are respected
- Spot workflow bottlenecks
- Maintain consistent board practices
