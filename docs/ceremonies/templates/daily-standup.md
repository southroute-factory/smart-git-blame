## Purpose

A daily 15-minute timebox for the Development Team to synchronize activities and create a plan for the next 24 hours. This meeting optimizes team collaboration and performance by inspecting progress toward the Sprint Goal.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Shared understanding of sprint progress
- Identification of blockers
- Coordination of daily work
- Updated sprint board
