## Purpose

The Sprint Review is a collaborative meeting held at the end of the sprint to inspect the Increment and adapt the Product Backlog if needed. The team demonstrates completed work to stakeholders and gathers feedback.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Demonstrate completed functionality
- Gather stakeholder feedback
- Update Product Backlog based on insights
- Celebrate team achievements
- Identify product direction adjustments
