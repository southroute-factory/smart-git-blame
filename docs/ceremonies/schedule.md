This document outlines the complete schedule of ceremonies and meetings for our two-week sprint cycle. Each meeting has a specific purpose and cadence to ensure effective team collaboration and continuous delivery.

## Ceremony Descriptions

### Sprint Planning
**Cadence:** First Monday of each sprint  
**Purpose:** Define the sprint goal and select backlog items for the upcoming sprint  
[View Template →](templates/sprint-planning.md)

### Daily Standup
**Cadence:** Every working day  
**Purpose:** Synchronize team activities and identify blockers  
[View Template →](templates/daily-standup.md)

### Sprint Review
**Cadence:** Last Friday of each sprint  
**Purpose:** Demonstrate completed work to stakeholders and gather feedback  
[View Template →](templates/sprint-review.md)

### Sprint Retrospective
**Cadence:** Last Friday of each sprint (after Sprint Review)  
**Purpose:** Reflect on the sprint process and identify improvements  
[View Template →](templates/sprint-retrospective.md)

### Backlog Refinement
**Cadence:** Weekly (Wednesday)  
**Purpose:** Review, estimate, and prepare backlog items for future sprints  
[View Template →](templates/backlog-refinement.md)

### Inbox Sort
**Cadence:** Weekly (Tuesday)  
**Purpose:** Triage and prioritize incoming requests and issues  
[View Template →](templates/inbox-sort.md)

### Project Board Review
**Cadence:** Weekly (Thursday)  
**Purpose:** Review work-in-progress and ensure board hygiene  
[View Template →](templates/project-board-review.md)

### Epic Kickoff
**Cadence:** As needed (when starting a new epic)  
**Purpose:** Align team on epic scope, goals, and initial breakdown  
[View Template →](templates/epic-kickoff.md)

## Best Practices

1. **Timeboxing:** Respect time allocations for all ceremonies
2. **Preparation:** Come prepared with necessary information
3. **Participation:** All team members should actively engage
4. **Focus:** Stay on topic and defer tangential discussions
5. **Documentation:** Use templates to capture key decisions and action items
6. **Follow-up:** Review and complete action items before next meeting

## Meeting Notes Storage

Meeting notes should be stored in `/docs/ceremonies/notes/` using the naming convention:
- `YYYY-MM-DD-{ceremony-type}.md`
- Example: `2024-01-15-sprint-planning.md`
